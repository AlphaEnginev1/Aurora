# Aurora Canonical Avatar Identity

**Status: CANONICAL**

`assets/aurora-central-avatar.jpg` is Aurora's canonical visual identity and central avatar.

## Identity contract

- The repository asset is the exact original Aurora image selected as the permanent visual reference.
- The source image must be preserved unchanged; replacing, altering, cropping, or stylistically transforming it requires explicit design authorization.
- The avatar is the central visual representation through which the human interacts with Aurora's Director.
- The future Aurora dashboard will use this canonical asset as the central presence of Aurora.
- Future voice and interaction work may animate the avatar in response to speech and system state (including movement, expression, attention, researching, planning, executing, waiting, discovering, learning, or requesting human input).
- Animation or motion must operate as a presentation layer around the canonical asset and must not silently replace the canonical source image.
- Voice, motion, and dashboard integration are future presentation work; this document does not authorize changes to the Aurora runtime, live-operation authorization, or the frozen production-readiness queue.
